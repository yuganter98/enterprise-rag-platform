LUMEN DYNAMICS - COMPENSATION GUIDELINES (CONFIDENTIAL: HR AND ADMIN ONLY)
Version 2026.1 (fictional company, sample data for demos)

1. SALARY BANDS (annual base salary, USD)
Software Engineer I: 85,000 - 105,000
Software Engineer II: 105,000 - 130,000
Senior Software Engineer: 130,000 - 165,000
Staff Engineer: 165,000 - 210,000
Engineering Manager: 150,000 - 195,000
Data Analyst: 75,000 - 98,000
HR Business Partner: 90,000 - 120,000

2. ANNUAL REVIEW CYCLE
Compensation reviews happen once a year in March. The total merit budget for 2026 is 4.5 percent of payroll. Employees rated "exceeds expectations" are eligible for a 6 to 9 percent increase; "meets expectations" for 3 to 5 percent.

3. PROMOTIONS
Promotions are decided by a calibration committee twice per year, in March and September. A promotion comes with a minimum 8 percent base salary increase and moves the employee at least to the bottom of the new band.

4. BONUSES
The annual company bonus target is 10 percent of base salary for individual contributors and 15 percent for managers, paid in February based on company and individual performance.

5. OFFERS
New-hire offers above the midpoint of the band require approval from the Head of People. Sign-on bonuses are capped at 20,000 USD.
